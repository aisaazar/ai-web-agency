# Sales Prospect Brief — City Rooms Nürnberg

Status: private research / outreach preparation only
Last checked: 2026-10-01

## Public facts

City Rooms presents centrally located rooms and apartments in Nürnberg and states that the property is one parallel street from the pedestrian zone, with the U-Bahn about one minute away and the main station about two minutes away. The site lists single, double, apartment, and family-room options.

Source: https://city-rooms-nuernberg.de/

The current public site footer identifies Worldsoft AG as the web-design provider, and the site's text includes a "Copyright: Cem Hür" line. The current Impressum identifies Dr.-Kurt-Schumacher-Straße 11, 90402 Nürnberg and names Romano Bonakdar as managing director.

Source: https://city-rooms-nuernberg.de/content/48/38/anfahrt-impressum

## Observable website opportunity

The page is content-rich but visually and structurally dated compared with a modern hospitality landing page: the extracted page is menu-heavy, uses a legacy-style footer/login structure, and does not expose a modern booking-focused hero or clear conversion hierarchy in the source structure. This is an observation for sales research, not a claim about business performance.

## Demo angle

Build a private redesign concept around:
- central Nürnberg location
- rooms + apartments
- fast access to U-Bahn and Hauptbahnhof
- clear room-category cards
- prominent booking/contact CTA
- mobile-first layout
- trust-focused local information
- modern visual hierarchy without changing factual claims
