## Observable presentation opportunity

The opportunity for a private redesign demo is therefore to preserve factual content while making the primary patient journey clearer: location, key services, who the practice is for, appointment CTA, and contact details.

This is a design observation, not a claim about conversion performance or business need.

## Private demo angle

Use a calm clinical / premium presentation:
- clear hero with local identity: Schwabach bei Nürnberg

- simple appointment CTA
- prominent phone contact

- clear legal/disclaimer surfaces
